Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

The short version

  • We don't have accounts, passwords, or a user database. Your key lives on your device or in the signer you choose.
  • What you post goes to Nostr relays run by other people, and is public unless the app tells you it's encrypted.
  • We run a few small helper services and a privacy-friendly analytics tool. You can turn analytics off in Settings.
  • No ads, no cookies, no selling or sharing your data.

Who we are

Coracle is a client for Nostr, an open, decentralized social network. This policy covers the Coracle app at app.coracle.social and the native Coracle apps, which are published by Coracle Social LLC (“we”).

Coracle is open source, and anyone can run their own copy. If you're using a copy hosted by someone else, they decide which services it talks to and this policy doesn't apply to them.

How Nostr changes the picture

On a traditional social network, one company stores everything. Nostr works differently: your identity is a cryptographic key pair, and your posts are signed messages stored on relays — independent servers run by many different people and organizations. Coracle is just the app you use to read and write those messages.

That has a few consequences worth knowing:

  • Posts, reactions, profiles, and follow lists are public. Anyone can read them, from any Nostr app.
  • Deletion isn't guaranteed. Coracle can ask relays to delete something, but each relay decides whether to honor that request, and copies may already exist elsewhere.
  • Encrypted messages hide their contents, not their existence. Relays can still see who the sender and recipient are and when a message was sent, depending on the type of message.
  • Relays see your IP address when Coracle connects to them, and each relay operator has its own policies.

What stays on your device

Coracle stores your settings, a cache of events it has downloaded, and your session in your browser's local storage (or the native app's storage). If you sign in with a private key, it is kept on your device only. If you use a signer — a browser extension, a remote signer like nsec.app, or a signing app on your phone — your key stays with that signer and Coracle only asks it to sign things.

Logging out removes your session and key from the device. Clearing your browser's site data (or uninstalling the app) removes everything else.

Services Coracle connects to

Here is every outside service the official app talks to, and what each one learns.

  • Nostr relays

    To load and publish events. Coracle uses the relays you've chosen, the relays of the people you interact with, and a few defaults: relay.damus.io, nos.lol, purplepag.es, nostr.wine, search.nos.today, relay.nsec.app, and ephemeral.snowflare.cc, plus indexer.coracle.social and bucket.coracle.social, which we run.

    Relays see what you request and publish, your public key, and your IP address.

  • Media servers (Blossom)

    When you attach a file, it is uploaded to your chosen media server, or by default to one of blossom.nostr.build, blossom.primal.net, cdn.satellite.earth, or void.cat. Files in encrypted messages are encrypted before they leave your device.

    The server sees the file (unless encrypted), your public key, and your IP address.

  • Dufflepud

    Our own helper service, hosted on Render. It fetches link previews, verifies Nostr addresses (NIP-05), looks up lightning addresses for zaps, and checks images from people you don't follow for sensitive content when “Hide sensitive content” is on. Routing these through Dufflepud means the sites behind them don't see your IP address — Dufflepud does instead.

    You can point Coracle at your own instance, or clear the setting to turn it off.

  • Analytics (Plausible)

    We run our own Plausible server at plausible.coracle.social to count page views. Before anything is sent, Nostr identifiers are stripped from the page address, so we can see that someone viewed “a profile” but not whose. Plausible uses no cookies and doesn't build profiles of visitors.

    Turn this off any time with “Report errors and analytics” in Settings.

  • Our web server

    The app is served from a server we run on Linode (Akamai), which also hosts the onboarding videos. Like most web servers, it logs the IP address, request, and response status of each request.

  • Content in notes

    Images, videos, and other media in notes load directly from wherever they're hosted. Spotify and Tidal links are shown as embedded players, which load from those companies. Each of these sees your IP address. You can set an image proxy in Settings to reduce this.

  • Lightning wallets

    If you connect a wallet to send zaps, payments go through that wallet and the recipient's lightning provider. We never hold your funds or see your wallet balance.

Coracle also links out to other sites, like njump.me for creating a new account, zap.stream for live streams, and Alby for wallet setup. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising, tracking pixels, or tracking cookies.
  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, or phone number.

Your choices and rights

Most of your Nostr data lives on relays we don't control, so the best way to manage it is through the app: edit your profile, delete posts, change relays, or switch off analytics and Dufflepud in Settings.

For the small amount we do hold — web server logs, aggregate analytics, and anything published to the relays we run — you can ask us to access or delete data related to you, wherever applicable law (such as the GDPR or CCPA) gives you that right. Email hello@coracle.social and we'll respond within a month.

Children

Coracle is for people 18 and older, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has shared information through a service we run, contact us and we'll remove it.

If we change how Coracle handles your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
